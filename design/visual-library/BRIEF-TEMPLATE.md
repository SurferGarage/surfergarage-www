## AI 视觉提示层

当确实需要生成背景或概念图时，使用以下固定前缀；人物报道与产品截图不得生成。

```text
Create a supporting visual for Surfer Garage, a media and open-learning brand for young technology founders.

Visual system:
- Deep Navy #060644, Electric Blue #0009E2, Cyan #27D7C7, Paper #F4F6FA
- physical wavefronts, documentary evidence, tracing paper, glass, resin or brushed metal
- cinematic directional light, tactile grain, strict editorial composition, clear negative space
- use only one brand motif per frame: wave OR signal OR node
- one focal idea only; the result should look photographed or art-directed, not procedurally assembled

Avoid:
- purple gradients, neon cyberpunk, glassmorphism, floating 3D blobs
- fake UI, fake data, fake people, stock-photo startup clichés
- visible grids and HUD decoration unless the content is genuinely data-led
- any logo or text generated inside the image; reserve clean zones for the official brand layer
```

然后追加具体用途、画幅、主体、留白位置与必须避免的元素。
